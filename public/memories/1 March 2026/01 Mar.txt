## Kalhan ki hikki 


That day humein kalhan ki hikki  ke baare mein pata chala, aur honestly hum uss situation ko ignore kar rahe the kyunki kaafi chaos chal raha tha. Phir metro mein humein aur cheezein pata chali, aur Bharat bhai ka phone bhi metro mein chori ho gaya. Uske baad hum Delhi metro station par the.
Tab tak trip almost end ho raha tha, aur mujhe suddenly realize hua ki hum dono ne apne attachment issues ke baare mein bhi baat ki thi. Jab hum kisi se attach ho jaate hain, toh usse handle karna humare liye kaafi mushkil ho jaata hai. 

Hum bas try karte rehte hain manage karne ke liye, but kabhi kabhi woh “too much” ho jaata hai at least mere liye toh definitely.

Phir humne alvida kaha, ek proper goodbye moment tha. Uske baad hum 8th March ko phir mile.
Overall, woh time honestly bahut hi achha tha Delhi ka woh phase finally uss din end hua, aur it was truly fabulous, wholesome, and genuinely great.
